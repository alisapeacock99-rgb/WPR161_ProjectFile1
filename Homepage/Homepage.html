An educational book is a written, published work that informs, explains, and narrates facts and ideas about a particular subject. Educational books can be in the form of textbooks, which are divided into different sections or subtopics. Books in the education nonfiction genre can either be books about forms of education and how it's achieved or be educational itself, in the form of textbooks and educational materials. The act of educating is to impart something in a learnable way to someone that does not yet have that knowledge.

Comics are a medium used to express ideas through images, often combined with text or other visual information. They typically take the form of a series of panels of images. Textual devices such as speech balloons, captions, and onomatopoeia can indicate dialogue, narration, sound effects, or other information. The size and arrangement of panels contribute to narrative pacing. Comics cover a wide range of genres and styles and can be serialized in magazines or presented in standalone formats like comic books or graphic novels.

Manhwa is the general Korean term for comics and print cartoons, specifically referring to South Korean comics. Similar to the Japanese word "manga", the Korean word "manhwa" derives from the Chinese term for comics, "manhua". Manhwa combines visual imagery with prose to deliver unique stories. It is a popular form of entertainment in South Korea and has spread to other nations.

Manga are comics or graphic novels originating from Japan. Most manga conform to a style developed in Japan in the late 19th century, and the form has a long history in earlier Japanese art. The term manga is used in Japan to refer to both comics and cartooning. Outside of Japan, the word is typically used to refer to comics originally published in Japan and published in translation, it is a Japanese comic book with English text.
